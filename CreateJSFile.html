2. Platform and activity information

Use of an online platform may generate technical and activity information, such as device or browser details, interaction records, security events and service diagnostics. The exact information collected depends on the features you use and the current implementation.

3. How information may be used

  • To operate and maintain account and platform features.
  • To review activity, verify eligible actions and administer rewards or withdrawals.
  • To help protect accounts, investigate suspicious activity and maintain platform security.

4. Service providers and integrations

Some platform features may rely on third-party services or integrations. Those providers may process information as needed to provide their services and are governed by their own terms and privacy information. Check the relevant provider's documentation for details.

5. Security and retention

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect account information. No online service can promise absolute security. Information should be retained only for as long as needed for legitimate platform, legal, security or dispute-resolution purposes.

6. Your choices and questions

For account or privacy questions, contact the platform support team at support@gyidibazar.com. Do not send passwords or sensitive authentication codes by email.
