FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Gyidi Bazar.

How do I withdraw my earnings?

+

You can withdraw via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Bank Transfer, or International Wire. Minimum withdrawal is 2,800 coins (approx $1 USD). Payments are auto-processed within 24 hours.

How long does receipt verification take?

+

Purchase receipts are verified within 24 hours using SHA-256 hash detection and EXIF timestamp validation to ensure authenticity. You will receive a notification once the coins are credited.

Is my personal data secure?

+

Yes. All user data is fully encrypted using AES-256 standards. We do not store full plain-text information. OTP verification and device fingerprinting add an extra layer of security, ensuring your data stays private.

Can I earn coins without purchasing anything?

+

Absolutely. You can earn coins for free through daily streaks, watching ads, spinning the luck wheel, and referring friends — no purchase required.

How does the 20% Affiliate Program work?

+

Once you refer a user via your link, they are permanently linked to you. You earn a 20% lifetime commission on every purchase they make across all 300+ partner brands. Commissions are auto-paid in PKR or USD.

What is the minimum withdrawal limit?

+

The minimum withdrawal limit is 2,800 Coins, which equals approximately $1 USD. We believe in low thresholds so you can access your earnings quickly and easily.

Are there any hidden fees or charges?

+

No. Gyidi Bazar does not charge any hidden fees for earning, verifying purchases, or withdrawing funds. What you earn is exactly what you get.

How do I contact support?

+

You can reach our 24/7 support team via the Dashboard Support tab or email us at support@gyidibazar.com. Premium members get priority support with a response time of under 2 hours.
